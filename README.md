# Jakob Grene

## Energy systems engineering portfolio

Projects from my studies at the University of Southern Denmark, covering energy modelling, optimization, electricity markets, forecasting and data analysis.

### Selected projects

- **[Electricity Price Forecasting with Transfer Learning](https://github.com/GreneGPU/electricity-price-forecasting-thesis)** — Master thesis on Nord Pool day-ahead price forecasting, transferring hourly models to 15-minute resolution.
- **[Energy Management of a Power-to-X Facility](https://github.com/GreneGPU/energy-management-optimization)** — Python models for power-to-X energy management, electricity-market bidding and sensitivity analysis.
- **[Optimal Charging and Discharging of Electric Vehicles](https://github.com/GreneGPU/advanced-energy-optimization)** — MATLAB optimization of EV charging schedules, arrival behaviour and renewable-aware electricity pricing.
- **[Simulation of Local Energy Communities](https://github.com/GreneGPU/technology-evaluation-simulation)** — Agent-based AnyLogic simulation of household PV, community wind generation and peer-to-peer electricity trading.
- **[New Heating Solutions at Gudbjerg and Lakkendrup](https://github.com/GreneGPU/bachelor-district-heating)** — Bachelor project comparing conventional and alternative district-heating solutions for two Danish towns.
- **[Fault Detection and Diagnosis in Engineering Systems](https://github.com/GreneGPU/fault-detection-diagnosis)** — MATLAB analysis of process faults using principal component analysis and dynamic monitoring methods.
